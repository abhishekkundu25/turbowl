inside its own job of the step, beside its sort, instead of on the caller's
thread before the jobs start. The code is kept on the branch
`exp/e10-index-in-job` (`3385461`), not landed. The kept version is

The implementer ran every suite on the change and all passed: the core

## Speed: lubm-50

Ten single runs per side on all cores and five on one thread, alternating, at
load 1.7 to 2.0. Reasoning in ms, medians:

| | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| All cores | 94.4 (middle half about 93.9 to 97.6) | 92.7 (about 91.9 to 97.5) |
| One thread | 387.4 | 386.7 |
| All cores against one thread | 4.10 | 4.17 |

The middle halves overlap: the 1.7 ms is within the noise, short of the 3 to
5 ms estimated, and the change was reverted. The time came off step 3, not
step 2. The likely reason is that `rdf:type`'s job, which now builds its 30 MB
index after its sort, is the step's critical path: before, that build had the
whole pool to itself. Peaks did not move (median 544.6 against 544.2 MB).
