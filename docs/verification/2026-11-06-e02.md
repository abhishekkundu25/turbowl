Reasoning times are within the spread everywhere. Loading got slower on the
large files: lubm-50's total went from 1.84 to 1.93 s and lubm-10's from 339
to 361 ms on one thread. The peak fell on every file larger than a window:
lubm-50 from 1,422 to 567 MB on one thread and from 1,616 to 643 MB on all
cores, lubm-10 from 269 to 134 MB.

What limits the peak now: the reasoner's own state after the run (39.5 bytes
per closure triple on all cores, 48.6 on one thread, where the store keeps
spare capacity; iteration 3) and, on all cores, 25.6 bytes per triple of
transient copies in the parallel step.
