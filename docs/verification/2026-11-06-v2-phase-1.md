The two inputs chaos found, both with an `owl:sameAs` that makes a
vocabulary term equal to `rdf:rest`:

- **Iteration 65821** (`owl:equivalentProperty` equal to `rdf:rest`):
  prp-key read a key list whose cells branch as sets of properties, and
  there are exponentially many of them. It took 32 s. Chains, as RDF lists
  are, keep their hash grouping; other shapes are read by pairs along the
  list's paths, as the reference evaluator does (`44e411a`, with a unit
  test and `scaling_identity_keys_on_a_branching_list`). It takes 0.16 s.
- **Iteration 27725** (`owl:sameAs` equal to `rdf:rest`): every cell of
  every list became an alias of every other, and of `rdf:nil`. The
  reference evaluator's list checks read each alias's pairs anew, member
  by member, for 29 s; this reasoner took 18 ms. Each head is now read once per
  diagnosis (`7a813b4`, with a test that pins the findings). The
  iteration takes 5.4 s, most of it in the reference's prp-key over the
  same aliases; a follow-up.

## The switch defaults

ADR 0005: a switch is off by default when other reasoners rarely implement
its rules, or when it slows reasoning by more than 5% on a scoreboard
scenario.

- **The research** (`docs/research/competitors-2026-11.md`, the rule table):
  owlrl, GraphDB's `owl2-rl` and the OWL-RS Nemo program implement both
  groups; Stardog has prp-key with `reasoning.sameas=FULL`. Jena has no
  `owl:hasKey` and no qualified cardinality, and balhoff's Soufflé program
  and `reasonable` have neither group. Neither is rarely implemented.
- **The measurement** (`B-*`): TurbOWL alone, the default configuration
  against the same with one switch off, 5 runs on the editor-size
  scenarios and 3 on the data-size ones. Reasoning time, median:

- **`schema` is off.** It slows 12 of the 15 scenarios by more than 5%.
  On LUBM it adds 99 triples at every size and costs 35% at every size, so
  its cost grows with the data, not only with the schema.
- **`identity` is off.** It slows uobm-010-u by 26%, where it grows the
  closure from 21.3 M to 39.1 M triples, and claros-l by 6% (91.0 M to
  114.4 M). It costs nothing measurable elsewhere.
- **What the defaults lose**: owlrl and GraphDB give key merges and schema
  subsumptions by default, and this reasoner no longer does (the spec's
  user story 16). Each group comes back with one switch.

The defaults are in `crates/turbowl/src/switch.rs`, and the reasons in the
crate doc, the CLI's `--switches` and the Python package's documentation.

## The W3C cases beyond the rules

Each case's premise and conclusion are in `data/w3c/owl2/profile-RL.rdf`.
The runner drops the conclusion's ontology header and its declarations
(`x a owl:Class` and the like), then needs every remaining triple in the
closure, with each blank node matched to any term. Each case's consistency
half passes, and its entailment half fails. IRIs are written by their local
names. "The rules" are OWL 2 Profiles §4.3, Tables 4 to 9.

**By refutation.** No rule concludes `owl:differentFrom` (only dt-diff, for
literals) or a type that is an `owl:complementOf` class. eq-diff1 to
eq-diff3, cls-com and the other checks only report a contradiction. So a
conclusion that holds because its opposite would be a contradiction is
beyond the rules:

- `DisjointClasses-001`: from `Boy owl:disjointWith Girl` and `Stewie a Boy`, expects `Stewie` typed by a class `owl:complementOf Girl`. Stewie as a Girl would fire cax-dw. No rule derives the complement type, and the premise has no class `owl:complementOf Girl` for the conclusion's blank node to match.
- `DisjointClasses-003`: the same with `owl:AllDisjointClasses (Boy Girl Dog)`. It expects `Stewie` in the complements of `Girl` and of `Dog` (cax-adc would fire).
- `New-Feature-ObjectQCR-002`: `Peter` has at most 1 `fatherOf` that is a `Woman` (`owl:maxQualifiedCardinality 1`), is father of `Stewie` and of `Meg`, a `Woman`, and `Stewie owl:differentFrom Meg`. It expects `Stewie` typed by `owl:complementOf Woman`: Stewie as a Woman would make cls-maxqc3 derive `Stewie owl:sameAs Meg`, and eq-diff1 fire. As above, no rule derives the type and no class matches.
- `New-Feature-DisjointObjectProperties-001`: from `hasFather owl:propertyDisjointWith hasMother`, `Stewie hasFather Peter` and `Stewie hasMother Lois`, expects `Peter owl:differentFrom Lois`. If the two were the same, prp-pdw would fire.
- `New-Feature-DisjointObjectProperties-002`: the same over three properties in `owl:AllDisjointProperties` (prp-adp would fire). It expects an `owl:AllDifferent` node with `owl:distinctMembers (Peter Lois StewieJr)`, and the node and its list are terms the premise does not have: no rule creates a term.
- `New-Feature-DisjointDataProperties-002`: three data properties in `owl:AllDisjointProperties` give the same literal, `"Peter Griffin"`, to `Peter`, `Peter_Griffin` and `Petre`. It expects `owl:AllDifferent` over the three, as the case above. It needs no datatype rule.
- `owl2-rl-rules-fp-differentFrom`: `fp` is functional, `Y1 fp X1`, `Y2 fp X2` and `X1 owl:differentFrom X2`. It expects `Y1 owl:differentFrom Y2`: if the two were the same, prp-fp would make `X1` and `X2` the same, and eq-diff1 fire.
- `owl2-rl-rules-ifp-differentFrom`: the converse: `ifp` is inverse functional, `Y1 ifp X1`, `Y2 ifp X2` and `Y1 owl:differentFrom Y2`. It expects `X1 owl:differentFrom X2`, through prp-ifp and eq-diff1.

**Datatypes.** No rule relates two datatypes. dt-type1 types each one
`rdfs:Datatype`, and no rule derives `rdfs:subClassOf` between datatypes or
compares their value spaces:

- `WebOnt-I5.8-006`: from `p rdfs:range xsd:byte`, expects `p rdfs:range xsd:short`. It needs `xsd:byte rdfs:subClassOf xsd:short`; with that triple, scm-rng1 (`schema`) would derive the range.
- `WebOnt-I5.8-008`: from the ranges `xsd:short` and `xsd:unsignedInt`, expects `p rdfs:range xsd:unsignedShort`. The two value spaces meet in 0 to 32,767, inside `xsd:unsignedShort`, but neither datatype is a subtype of it: the conclusion needs the intersection of two value spaces.
- `WebOnt-I5.8-009`: from the ranges `xsd:nonNegativeInteger` and `xsd:nonPositiveInteger`, expects `p rdfs:range xsd:short`. The two value spaces meet only in 0, a short: again an intersection of value spaces.

**A new term.**

- `WebOnt-I5.5-005`: from the declaration of one class `a`, expects a class `owl:unionOf (a)`: a blank node and a one-cell list. The case's description names OWL Full's comprehension principles. No rule creates a term, so no class or list in the closure matches.

**No rule in the tables.**

- `WebOnt-differentFrom-001`: from `a owl:differentFrom b`, expects `b owl:differentFrom a`. The relation is symmetric in the semantics, but no rule derives `owl:differentFrom` from `owl:differentFrom`.
- `New-Feature-ReflexiveProperty-001`: from `knows a owl:ReflexiveProperty` and `Peter a owl:NamedIndividual`, expects `Peter knows Peter`. No rule reads `owl:ReflexiveProperty`.
- `chain2trans1`: from `p owl:propertyChainAxiom (p p)`, expects `p a owl:TransitiveProperty`. The chain means the same as transitivity, and prp-spo2 applies it to every two `p` triples, but the premise has none, and no rule concludes a property characteristic.

**The runner, not the rules.** Three cases need the case read in a way the
runner does not:

- `WebOnt-imports-011`: from `Socrates a Man` and `owl:imports support011-A`, expects `Socrates a Mortal`. The imported ontology is in the export (`test:importedOntology`) and holds `Man rdfs:subClassOf Mortal`, from which cax-sco gives the conclusion. The runner loads the premise alone, and no rule reads `owl:imports`. It needs the imported ontology loaded with the premise.
- `WebOnt-I4.6-005-Direct`: from `C1 owl:equivalentClass C2` and `C1 rdfs:comment "An example class."`, expects `C2 rdfs:comment "An example class."`. The case's regime is the Direct Semantics only, which ignores annotations in the conclusion (its description says so), so all it asks is `C2`'s declaration. No rule copies an annotation across `owl:equivalentClass`, and the RDF-Based twin, `WebOnt-I4.6-005`, is a non-entailment test that passes. It needs the runner to drop the annotations from a Direct-only conclusion.
- `WebOnt-equivalentClass-008-Direct`: the same with the annotation property `annotate`: from `c1 owl:equivalentClass c2` and `c1 annotate "description of c1"`, expects `c2 annotate "description of c1"`. Its RDF-Based twin, `WebOnt-equivalentClass-008`, passes.
